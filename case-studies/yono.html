Redesigning YONO SBI

A redesign exploration of India's most-used banking app — simplifying navigation, improving financial clarity, and building a more trustworthy experience for everyday users.

TypeUI Redesign Exploration
DomainFintech · Banking
ToolsFigma

Approach

YONO SBI serves millions of users daily but its interface has grown complex over time. This redesign exploration focused on decluttering the home screen, improving information hierarchy, and making financial data more digestible for the average user.

Simplified navigation with clear primary actions and contextual secondary options.
Improved visual hierarchy for account balances, recent transactions, and pending actions.
Consistent card-based layout for services reduces cognitive load when switching between features.
Enhanced readability through better typography, spacing, and contrast ratios.
